Famous, but left out (too far for this route)

  • Bath, Stonehenge, Oxford and Cambridge: each needs a day trip. Bath and Stonehenge pair well with a car; Oxford is about 1 h from London by train and is a fine swap on Day 14.
  • Isle of Skye, Ben Nevis and the Jacobite steam train: need a long day or an overnight in the west Highlands. Day 12 has them as alternatives.
  • Lake District, York and Edinburgh's Festival season (August): not on this route. York is an easy stopover on Day 13.
  • World-famous brands, England. Too far: Rolls-Royce (Goodwood), Aston Martin and Jaguar Land Rover (Midlands factory tours, advance booking), Wedgwood (Stoke). Day trip: Cadbury World (Bournville, Birmingham). Easy shop stops: Burberry and Fortnum & Mason (Piccadilly), Twinings tea (The Strand), Liberty, Hamleys and Harrods. Take home Marmite, Cadbury and Twinings.
  • World-famous brands, Scotland. Easy shop stops in Edinburgh: Johnnie Walker (Princes Street flagship), Royal Mile Whiskies, Walker's Shortbread, Scottish cashmere and tartan on the Royal Mile. Day trip: Glenfiddich and other Speyside distilleries are far north, and Edradour near Pitlochry may fit a Highlands tour stop on Day 11.
  • Already covered: the Harry Potter sites (Leadenhall, Platform 9¾, Victoria Street, Glenfinnan, Gloucester Cathedral), the Koh-i-Noor, and Dr Ambedkar's London house.

Check these before booking

  • Day tours (Days 6, 7, 11, 12): book 2–4 weeks ahead. The Cotswold buses (such as the 801) change timetable often, so check traveline.info and the operator pages.
  • Jacobite steam train / Glenfinnan: sources conflict on the 2027 season. Treat the dates as unconfirmed until West Coast Railways announces them.
  • Cheltenham to Edinburgh direct train is about 6 hours and runs only a few times a day. Confirm it on National Rail, and compare it with a change at Birmingham.
  • Seasonal openings: Sudeley Castle, Hidcote and heritage railways vary by date. Buckingham Palace State Rooms open only in summer, and the Changing of the Guard is on selected days only.
  • Daylight and weather: London has about 14 h of daylight in mid-April, and Scotland has 15–17 h by May. Highland weather is changeable, so pack layers and a waterproof. Midges (biting flies) usually arrive from late May or June.
  • Windsor and LEGOLAND (Day 15): LEGOLAND is seasonal and does not open every day in spring, so check its calendar first. Windsor Castle can close for state events, and St George's Chapel is closed to visitors on Sundays.
  • Events: Beltane Fire Festival (Edinburgh, 30 April), the Cheltenham Jazz Festival (usually early May) and the Chelsea Flower Show (late May) can raise hotel prices.

London · Days 1–4

Arrival, royal London, the City, museums and markets. Currency: GBP (£). Contactless cards work on the Tube and buses.

Cheltenham and the Cotswolds · Days 5–8

A Regency spa town, honey-stone villages and real-ale pubs. Mixed transport: train, bus and taxi.

Edinburgh and Scotland · Days 9–12

Edinburgh's Old Town, then two Highland day tours. Scotland is part of the UK, so no extra visa.

Back to London · Days 13–16

The train south, Notting Hill, Greenwich, a Windsor day trip with LEGOLAND, then the flight home.

Essentials

Getting there and away (Mumbai)

  • Nonstop Mumbai ↔ London Heathrow flights run about 9–10 h (Air India, British Airways and Virgin Atlantic have operated this route, so check current schedules). Because the trip starts and ends in London, a simple return ticket works and no open-jaw is needed.
  • Visa: Indian passport holders need a UK Standard Visitor visa (apply online on gov.uk and submit biometrics at the visa application centre). It covers England and Scotland. Apply 2–3 months ahead and check current fees and processing time. The UK is not in the Schengen area.

Money and getting around

  • Cards and Apple/Google Pay are accepted nearly everywhere, and contactless works on the Tube and buses. Carry very little cash.
  • Apps: Trainline (UK trains), TfL Go or Citymapper (London), Lothian Buses (Edinburgh), traveline (rural buses).
  • Book in advance: the Paddington to Cheltenham and Cheltenham to Edinburgh trains, the Edinburgh to King's Cross train (LNER), the Tower of London, Westminster Abbey, Edinburgh Castle, the Highland day tours and the Sky Garden.
  • Consider a Railcard only if you qualify. Advance train tickets are the cheapest option.
  • Tap water is safe everywhere. Bring a refillable bottle. Tipping is about 10–12.5% in restaurants if service is not included.

Must-try food, quick list

  • England: fish and chips, Sunday roast with Yorkshire pudding, pie and mash, cream tea (scone, clotted cream and jam), ploughman's lunch, a full English breakfast, and a curry in Brick Lane or at Dishoom.
  • Gloucestershire: Double Gloucester cheese, Gloucester Old Spot pork, local ales, cider and perry.
  • Scotland: haggis, neeps and tatties, cullen skink, Scotch pie, shortbread, tablet, cranachan, Irn-Bru, and a whisky tasting. Vegetarian haggis is widely available.
